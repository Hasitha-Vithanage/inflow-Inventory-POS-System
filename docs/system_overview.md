# InFlow - System Modules and Features Overview

This document provides a comprehensive list of all modules and features within the InFlow system, explaining the capabilities of each.

---

## 1. Dashboard
The central hub for monitoring system performance and key metrics.
*   **Total KPIs**: View real-time summaries of Sales, Purchases, Returns, and Expenses.
*   **Visual Analytics**: Graphical representations (charts) of sales and payment trends.
*   **Recent Activity**: Quick view of recent sales and transactions.
*   **Stock Alerts**: Instant visibility into products that are low in stock.
*   **Warehouse Filtering**: Filter dashboard data by specific warehouses for localized insights.

## 2. Inventory & Product Management
A robust system for handling the product lifecycle and stock tracking.
*   **Product Catalog**: Manage simple, variant (size, color), and combo products.
*   **Barcode System**: Auto-generate barcodes or use existing ones; print labels with customizable sizes and price visibility.
*   **Stock Control**: Track real-time stock levels across multiple warehouses.
*   **Initial Stock Import**: Easily set opening stock levels when creating products or via Excel import.
*   **Category & Brand Management**: Organize products with hierarchical categories, subcategories, and brands.
*   **Unit Management**: Define base and sub-units (e.g., Box of 12) for flexible selling.
*   **Stock Count**: Perform physical inventory audits and reconcile differences.

## 3. Sales & Point of Sale (POS)
Streamlined workflows for handling customer transactions.
*   **Point of Sale (POS)**: A modern, clean interface optimized for speed; supports barcode scanners, touchscreen, and double-screen customer displays.
*   **Offline POS Sync**: Continue selling even without internet; data syncs automatically when reconnected.
*   **Multi-Payment Support**: Accept payments via multiple methods (Cash, Card, Transfer) in a single transaction.
*   **Sales Management**: Create, edit, and track sales invoices with status (Paid, Partial, Unpaid).
*   **Sales Returns**: Process returns based on the original sale reference to maintain stock integrity.
*   **Sale Drafts**: Save incomplete transactions to resume later.
*   **Quotation System**: Create professional quotes for customers and convert them to sales with one click.
*   **Shipments**: Manage delivery status and shipping details for sales.
*   **Attachments**: Upload and link documents (e.g., signed delivery notes) to sales.

## 4. Purchases & Supply Chain
Manage incoming stock and relationships with suppliers.
*   **Purchase Orders**: Create and track orders from suppliers.
*   **Purchase Returns**: Manage returns to suppliers with automatic stock deduction.
*   **Supplier Management**: Maintain detailed profiles, tax numbers, and transaction history for providers.
*   **Purchase Documents**: Attach invoices and receipts directly to purchase records.

## 5. Warehouse & Inventory Movements
Precise control over stock location and movement.
*   **Warehouse Management**: Assign specific warehouses to users for restricted access.
*   **Stock Transfers**: Move items between warehouses with a mandatory manager approval workflow.
*   **Inventory Adjustments**: Manually add or subtract stock for corrections (e.g., found items).
*   **Damage Management**: Track and deduct damaged items from inventory.

## 6. Accounting Module (V2)
A complete double-entry accounting system for financial clarity.
*   **Chart of Accounts (CoA)**: Hierarchical account structure for Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, and Expenses.
*   **Journal Entries**: Record manual financial transactions.
*   **Financial Reports**: Generate Trial Balances, Profit & Loss statements, and Balance Sheets.
*   **Cash Flow Tracking**: Monitor inflows and outflows by account or payment method.
*   **VAT & Tax Reporting**: Automatic summaries of tax collected and paid from transactions.

## 7. HRM (Human Resource Management)
Manage your workforce and payroll efficiently.
*   **Employee Records**: Centralized database of staff with roles, departments, and designations.
*   **Attendance Tracking**: Daily and monthly attendance logs with total worked hours.
*   **Leave Management**: Manage employee leave requests and types.
*   **Payroll**: Generate and manage employee salaries and payouts.
*   **Holiday Calendar**: Define company holidays for attendance planning.

## 8. Customer & Client Relations
Tools for maintaining strong customer loyalty and financial tracking.
*   **Customer Profiles**: Store detailed info, custom fields, and tax numbers.
*   **Client Ledger**: Complete history of a customer's sales, payments, and returns.
*   **Credit Limits**: Set and enforce credit limits for partial or unpaid sales.
*   **Loyalty Points System**: Reward customers with points based on their purchase activity.
*   **Client Opening Balance**: Handle outstanding balances when migrating to the system.

## 9. Specialized Modules
*   **Service & Maintenance**: Manage service jobs, technicians, and dynamic checklist templates.
*   **Project & Task Management**: Organize work into projects with Kanban boards, discussions, and document sharing.
*   **Asset Management**: Register and track company assets (equipment, vehicles, etc.).
*   **Booking Module**: Manage customer appointments and bookings.
*   **E-commerce integration**: Sync products and orders with WooCommerce or use the built-in Online Store.

## 10. Reporting & Analytics
Over 50+ reports for deep business intelligence.
*   **Sales Reports**: By Category, Brand, Seller, and Payment Method.
*   **Inventory Reports**: Stock Aging, Dead Stock, Zero Sales, and Negative Stock alerts.
*   **Financial Reports**: Expense/Deposit summaries, Profit Net (FIFO or Average Cost).
*   **Customer/Supplier Reports**: Inactive customers, Top Suppliers, and Due Amount reports.

## 11. System Configuration & Security
*   **Role-Based Access (ACL)**: Define granular permissions for different users.
*   **Security Settings**: Manage active login devices and view login activity reports.
*   **Appearance Customization**: Change logos, favicons, colors, and login page text without coding.
*   **Integrations**: Connect with SMS gateways (Twilio, Nexmo, Termii), Email, Stripe, and QuickBooks.
*   **Cloud Backups**: Auto-store database backups on Google Drive, Dropbox, or S3.
